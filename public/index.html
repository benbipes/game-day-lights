Easy 2-Step Philips Hue Setup

  1. Enter or Auto-Detect your Hue Bridge IP:
    Click 🔍 Auto-Detect IP or open the Philips Hue App on your iPhone ➔ Settings ➔ Bridge settings ➔ Network settings to find your Bridge's local IP address (e.g. 192.168.1.50).
  2. Pair with the Bridge:
    Walk over to your physical Philips Hue Bridge and press the large round button on top. Then immediately click 🔘 Pair Bridge above within 30 seconds. The app will automatically generate and save your secure Hue Application Key!
  3. Select your Room / Entertainment Zone:
    The app will list all your Hue rooms (Living Room, TV Area, etc.). Pick the room you want to light up during game day!
🍏 Apple Home Hub Note: Because your Philips Hue lights are already synced with your Apple Home hub, any changes triggered here are automatically reflected throughout your Apple Home ecosystem!
